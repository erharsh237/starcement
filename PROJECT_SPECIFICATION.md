### 2.2 Certified Cement Product Specifications

Conforming to **Ghana Standards Authority GS 1118:2024** and **EN 197-1**, Star Cement manufactures strictly two certified formulations:

#### Product 1: Star Cement CEM II/A-L 42.5R (Rapid-Strength Structural Engineering)
- **Standard**: GS 1118:2024 / EN 197-1
- **Classification**: Portland Limestone Cement (CEM II / A-L)
- **Grade**: 42.5R (Rapid Hardening)
- **Tagline**: Rapid-strength Portland Limestone Cement for fast-paced construction, high early strength, and quicker formwork removal.
- **Physical & Mechanical Properties**:
  - **2-Day Early Compressive Strength**: ≥ 20.0 MPa (Typically 22.0 – 25.0 MPa)
  - **28-Day Final Compressive Strength**: ≥ 42.5 MPa (Typically 46.0 – 52.0 MPa)
  - **Initial Setting Time**: ≥ 60 minutes (Typically 110 – 140 minutes)
  - **Soundness (Expansion)**: ≤ 10 mm (Typically 1.0 – 2.0 mm)
  - **Specific Surface (Blaine Fineness)**: ≥ 340 m²/kg (Typically 380 – 410 m²/kg)
- **Recommended Structural Applications**:
  - Reinforced concrete building frames, suspended slabs, columns, and transfer beams.
  - High-rise raft foundations, cast-in-place retaining walls, and civil substructures.
  - Precast bridge girders, culverts, prestressed concrete elements, and transmission poles.
  - Ready-mix commercial batching requiring accelerated formwork stripping cycles.

#### Product 2: Star Cement CEM II/B-L 32.5R (Versatile Masonry & General Purpose)
- **Standard**: GS 1118:2024 / EN 197-1
- **Classification**: Portland Limestone Cement (CEM II / B-L)
- **Grade**: 32.5R (High Early Set)
- **Tagline**: Versatile and economical Portland Limestone Cement for block making, masonry works, plastering, rendering, and general construction.
- **Physical & Mechanical Properties**:
  - **2-Day Early Compressive Strength**: ≥ 10.0 MPa (Typically 13.0 – 16.0 MPa)
  - **28-Day Final Compressive Strength**: ≥ 32.5 MPa (Typically 35.0 – 40.0 MPa)
  - **Initial Setting Time**: ≥ 75 minutes (Typically 130 – 170 minutes)
  - **Soundness (Expansion)**: ≤ 10 mm (Typically 1.0 – 2.5 mm)
  - **Specific Surface (Blaine Fineness)**: ≥ 320 m²/kg (Typically 350 – 380 m²/kg)
- **Recommended Applications & Typical Bag Yields**:
  - **Commercial Sandcrete Block Making**:
    - **5" Hollow Blocks (125mm)**: 35 – 40 blocks / 50kg bag (1 bag : 3.5 – 4 wheelbarrows quarry sand)
    - **6" Hollow Blocks (150mm)**: 28 – 32 blocks / 50kg bag (1 bag : 3.0 – 3.5 wheelbarrows sharp sand)
    - **9" Solid Blocks (225mm)**: 18 – 22 blocks / 50kg bag (1 bag : 2.5 – 3.0 wheelbarrows clean coarse sand)
  - **Smooth Wall Plastering & Rendering**: 1 bag : 4 wheelbarrows sand (high moisture retention, silky troweling, zero shrinkage crazing).
  - **Masonry Bedding Mortar**: 1 bag : 3.5 wheelbarrows sand (high tensile grip and joint cohesion).
  - **Residential General Concrete**: Ground slabs, surface aprons, boundary fences, and drainage gutters.

> **Product Exclusion Note**: CEM I 52.5N (Obotan) is decommissioned and not produced.

## 4. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Feature Set

### 4.1 Client-Facing MVP Capabilities
1. **Full Brand & Brochure Synchronization**:
   - Complete incorporation of Star Cement's official brochure across all pages.
   - Zero outdated references to decommissioned products or inaccurate capacities.
2. **Interactive Dealer Directory**:
   - Comprehensive directory of 15+ authorized distributors across Greater Accra, Ashanti, Western, Central, and Eastern regions.
   - Instant filtering by region and search by city/dealer name.
   - One-touch mobile dialing and pre-configured WhatsApp commercial dispatch ordering.
3. **Interactive Mix & Block Yield Calculator**:
   - Practical job-site estimator for Ghanaian contractors and commercial block makers.
   - Computes bag counts, sand barrows, and granite requirements from slab dimensions.
4. **Command Palette (`Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K`)**:
   - Power-user keyboard shortcut for jumping across the entire website instantly.
5. **Commercial Dispatch Quote System**:
   - Instant inquiry submission modal generating unique procurement tracking references (`STAR-GH-XXXXXX`).
6. **Mobile-First Responsive Layout**:
   - Full mobile navigation drawer with expandable accordions, quick-action hotlines, and dealer links.

### 4.2 Architectural Resilience & Deployment Readiness
- **Zero-Dependency Static Build**: Complete client-side fallback data structures (`dealers.js`, `plants.js`, `products.js`, `salesReps.js`). The website runs 100% functional with zero errors even when deployed as a static bundle without an active database.
- **Vercel Deployment Ready**: Configured with `vercel.json` SPA rewrites, allowing direct deep-linking and browser page refreshes without 404 errors.
- **Optional Full-Stack Backend**: Express 5.2 backend supporting PostgreSQL / Supabase with local JSON store fallback for commercial inquiry persistence.
